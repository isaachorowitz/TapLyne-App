Give your AI a real iPhone.

TapLyne lets an AI agent see, tap, scroll and type on the iPhone plugged into your Mac. The screen comes in over USB. The taps go out over Bluetooth. Nothing gets installed on the phone.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Free and open source. macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Works with Claude Code, any MCP client, or REST.

  1. USB carries the screen.Captured locally on your Mac.
  2. Bluetooth carries the taps.AssistiveTouch turns them into touches.
  3. Nothing on the iPhone.No app, no jailbreak.
  4. You stay in charge.Pause or take over any time.
